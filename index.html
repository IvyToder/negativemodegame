Your mission: make negative sentences accurately. Read the clues, watch the subject, and choose the tense that matches the meaning.

🗓️ Simple Present

I / you / we / they + don't + base verb
he / she / it + doesn't + base verb
Mia doesn't eat meat.
Habits, routines and things that are generally true.

⏱️ Present Continuous

am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing
Mia isn't eating right now.
Actions happening now or around this moment.
Golden rule: After don't or doesn't, use the base verb: doesn't like — not doesn't likes.
